Install Docker

Docker is installed from your edition's own packages. It can take a few minutes, on Gentoo much longer: it is compiled.

Where containers live

Containers, images and volumes go on the datasets <pool>/containers/docker and <pool>/containers/containerd, outside the system: a rollback of the system leaves them alone, and the pool's snapshots and replication take them along. What Docker already holds is copied there.

Choose the pool once: moving containers to another pool later is not possible yet.
